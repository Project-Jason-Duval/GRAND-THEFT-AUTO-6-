Confirmed regions Official

Vice City

Confirmed

The "sun and fun capital of America." Ocean Beach brings white sand and pastel art deco; the Tisha-Wocka flea market deals in premium-grade bootleg luxuries. Glamour, hustle, and greed, all in one skyline.

Leonida Keys

Confirmed

A tropical archipelago and Vice City's laid-back counterpart — island-hopping, diving, and boat parties under a casual dress code. Part of Jason's own backstory.

Grassrivers

Confirmed

Leonida's marshlands — swampy, lush, and not without teeth. Alligators and dense cover make it prime territory for off-road racing and staying lost.

Port Gellhorn

Confirmed

A coastal town worn down by neglect — cheap motels, rundown attractions, and locals who don't take kindly to outsiders. Not the place to relax.

Ambrosia

Confirmed

Leonida's industrial backbone, built on sugar wealth and thick factory smoke — with a biker gang proud of "America's good old days" running most of the rest.

Mount Kalaga

Confirmed

A national park built around Leonida's tallest peak — rivers, forests, and wildlife, popular with hunters and off-roaders alike.

FAQ

How do the countdowns work?

Each timer reads the current time from your device, compares it to a fixed target moment, and updates every second. It never shows a negative number — once a target passes, the timer shows a status message instead.

Why do the timers use UTC?

UTC doesn't change with daylight saving or your location, so a countdown built on it stays correct for every visitor, everywhere. Your device still converts the final displayed values using its own clock, so the day/hour/minute/second counts are already correct for you — no manual conversion needed.

What is OBS mode?

Add ?mode=obs to the page URL to hide the navigation, hero, and footer and show a transparent background with enlarged countdown digits — built for dropping this page into a streaming overlay. Timers and progress bars keep working normally.

Where is my data stored?

Everything you add — watchlist entries, locations, trailers, screenshots, news, theories, and summary rows — is saved in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing is shared between devices or browsers.

Can I change the countdown dates?

Yes. Open script.js and edit the EVENTS object near the top of the file — each date is written with Date.UTC(year, monthIndex, day, hour, minute, second), where January is month 0.

How do I add real trailers, screenshots, or images?

Use the forms in the Trailers and Screenshots sections to paste a trusted video or image URL. Until you do, those cards show a clearly labeled placeholder instead of a broken embed — this site never invents a video ID or image link.

Can I add more sections?

Yes — duplicate a <section> block in index.html, give it a unique id, add a matching link in the navigation, and style or script it the same way the existing sections are handled.

How do I clear my saved data?

Use the "Clear saved data" button below. It asks for confirmation, then removes only this site's saved information — nothing else in your browser is touched.

How do I deploy this website?

Keep index.html, styles.css, and script.js in the same folder and open index.html in a browser, or upload the folder to any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or a plain web server). No build step or backend is required.
